R4 ->
viewDidLoad:
- it is called when the view controller's view is loaded into memory.
- it is generally called only once during lifetime of that loaded view.
- UI elements , buttons , labels , and initial constraints should be configured here
- Initial data can also be loaded here

viewWillAppear:
- it is called every time view controller is about to become visible
- it can be called multiple times when navigating between screens
- useful for resfreshing information that may have changed on another screen

I used viewDidLoad for initial UI setup and viewWillAppear for tasks that may need refreshing whenever screem appears
